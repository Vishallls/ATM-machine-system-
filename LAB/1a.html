Department Of Electronics and Communication Engineering

Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) is the backbone of contemporary communication systems and stands as the cornerstone of modern communication systems, governing myriad aspects in the contemporary era. It is governing anything to everything in the current era. The telecom sector is growing at a very fast rate, solidifying ECE as an essential branch of engineering for sustainability and progress. The evolving technologies: Internet of Things (IoT), Industrial IoT (IIoT), Augmented reality/virtual reality (AR/VR), AI/ML, haptics, edge offloading, edge computing, nano scale manufacturing , fabrication and the like, heavily rely on the ECE branch of engineering. From the design to fabrication of components/electronic devices, to their communication, everything lies under the curriculum of the ECE programs.

Thrust Areas of the Department

  • Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Embedded Systems
  • RF and Microwave Engineering
  • Semiconductor Devices
  • VLSI Circuits and Systems

Advanced Research Facilities

The department of Electronics and Communication has exceptionally well-equipped state-of-the-art facilities in its primary research fields owing to its significant focus on advanced and current experimental research in applied electronics and technological development activities. The department has recently been sanctioned a 5G use case lab by the Department of Telecom (DoT), Government of India, to pursue next generation research in the domain of 5G and 6G communication. It is one of the 100 labs set-up in the country. Apart from the new communication lab, the department has a Fabrication lab also, to create an end-to-end semiconductor ecosystem for budding researchers
